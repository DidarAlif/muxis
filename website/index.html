Frequently Asked Questions

Key answers about MUXIS platforms, audio formats, privacy, and cloud sync.

What operating systems does the MUXIS music player support?

MUXIS by A Leaf ORG provides native releases for Windows 10/11 (.exe and portable .zip), Android (.apk and .aab), macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Linux (GTK3 with PulseAudio/PipeWire), and iPhone/iPad (.ipa).

Which audio formats and DSP equalizer features are built into MUXIS?

MUXIS supports lossless FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC, M4A, OGG, and Opus alongside a hardware-accelerated 10-band parametric equalizer (32Hz to 16kHz), Bass Boost, Treble Air, Pre-Amp Gain, Slowed + Reverb, and Karaoke Vocal Cut.

Is MUXIS free, open source, and private?

Yes. MUXIS is distributed under the GNU GPL-3.0 open-source license with zero advertisements and zero tracking telemetry. Your audio files stay local on your own device.

How does MUXIS Cloud sync work across Windows, Android, macOS, and Linux?

Optional 1-Click Google Sign-In via Firebase and encrypted Cloudflare Workers + Cloudflare D1 synchronize playlists, favorite tracks, listening history, 10-band EQ curves, and studio themes across devices.
