Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 28, 2026

1. Local Audio Stays Strictly On Your Device

MUXIS is architected as an offline-first audio player. Your local music files, folders, storage scans, and personal recordings are processed solely on your device and are never uploaded or shared.

2. MUXIS Cloud Authentication & Cloudflare D1 Sync

When you optionally sign in using 1-Click Google Sign-In (powered by Firebase Authentication) or email credentials, MUXIS stores only user-created playlist titles, favorite track metadata, listening history entries, 10-band EQ presets, and visual theme settings inside encrypted Cloudflare Workers + Cloudflare D1 storage.

You can sign out, export, or delete your MUXIS Cloud data directly inside Settings → Cloud Account at any time.

3. Zero Ad Networks & Zero Telemetry

MUXIS by A Leaf does not sell personal information, bundle third-party advertising SDKs, or run behavioral tracking beacons.
