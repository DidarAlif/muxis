Privacy Policy

Built as an offline-first music player by A Leaf ORG with zero tracking telemetry or data monetization.

1. Local Audio Never Leaves Your Device

Your local music library files, folders, storage scans, and audio files stay strictly on your own hardware.

2. Optional Cloud Sync (Firebase + Cloudflare D1)

If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication) or email credentials, MUXIS stores only user playlists, favorites, listening history metadata, EQ presets, and visual theme settings inside encrypted Cloudflare Workers & Cloudflare D1 storage.

3. Zero Ads & Zero Trackers

MUXIS contains no third-party advertising networks or behavioral telemetry SDKs.
