Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Core Privacy Principle

Muxis is designed as an offline-first music player. Your local music library files, audio recordings, and local directories are never uploaded, analyzed, or shared with cloud services or third parties.

2. Cloud Synchronization Data

If you create an account, Muxis synchronizes only user-created playlist titles, favorite track metadata, and application preferences using Supabase PostgreSQL protected by Row Level Security (RLS). You may delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time directly within the app.

3. No Tracking & No Telemetry

Muxis does not sell user data, bundle analytics trackers, or display third-party advertisements.
