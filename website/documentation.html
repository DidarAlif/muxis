MUXIS Documentation

Guide to whole-storage music discovery, 10-band DSP, Firebase + Cloudflare D1 cloud sync, and desktop keyboard shortcuts.

1. Importing Local Music & Full-Storage Scan

Tap Import Music in Library to scan local folders across .mp3, .flac, .wav, .aac, .m4a, .ogg, and .opus.

Android & iPhone: Android scans internal storage and SD cards recursively; iPhone scans Files → On My iPhone → MUXIS → Music or any user-picked iOS directory.

2. Audio Studio & 10-Band Parametric DSP

Adjust gain across 32Hz – 16kHz bands alongside Studio Presets (Prismatic Neon, Sub Bass Thump, Vocal Clarity, Electronic EDM, Flat), Sub-Bass Drive, Treble Air, Pre-Amp Gain, and Master Bypass.

3. MUXIS Cloud (Firebase Auth + Cloudflare D1)

Authenticate with 1-Click Google Sign-In powered by Firebase Authentication or email credentials. Playlists, favorites, listening history, 10-band EQ curves, and studio themes sync over encrypted Cloudflare Workers + D1 storage while audio files remain local.
